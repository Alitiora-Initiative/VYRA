# VYRA Markets — App Store Listing Draft (Paper Prototype Only)

**State:** Draft only; no store submission or native app build exists.

## Name
VYRA Markets

## Short description
Explore multi-asset markets and practise with a paper account.

## Full description
VYRA Markets is a market-information and paper-trading workspace.

Explore provider-sourced market snapshots, organise your own watchlist, review instrument details and test hypothetical market or limit orders using a simulated paper account. Review open orders, positions and an auditable paper ledger in one responsive workspace.

The current VYRA prototype does not accept deposits or withdrawals, hold customer assets, provide live execution, or open a real brokerage account. Paper balances and simulated fills are not real money, investment returns or offers of financial products. Data availability, freshness and redistribution rights are subject to the applicable provider terms.

No financial, investment, tax or legal advice is provided.

## Non-negotiable review gate
Do not submit this draft as a live brokerage offering. Google Play requires organisation developer accounts for financial products such as trading and funds. Apple states financial trading/investing apps should be submitted by the financial institution providing the services and that the required permissions must exist in each storefront jurisdiction. The draft must be revisited after a regulated provider contract, legal classification, country matrix and partner app approval are complete.
