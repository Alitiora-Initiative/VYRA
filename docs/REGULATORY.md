# Regulatory and operating boundary

VYRA is currently a software prototype with a paper-trading engine. It is not authorised to accept client funds, custody assets, offer securities/derivatives/FX brokerage, or execute real trades.

The legal classification and licensing obligations depend on the legal entity, product, execution model, custody model, marketing, target customers and every country in which the service operates. No assumption is made that one licence or broker integration covers all asset classes or jurisdictions.

Before any live-money launch, obtain jurisdiction-specific advice and written provider confirmation for:
- operating entity, permissions, passporting/cross-border restrictions and marketing;
- client classification, KYC, AML, sanctions, source-of-funds and ongoing monitoring;
- safeguarding/segregation, custody, reconciliation and insolvency treatment;
- appropriateness/suitability and product disclosures where applicable;
- order handling, best execution, conflicts, complaints and records retention;
- tax reporting, privacy/data protection, outsourcing and cyber resilience;
- capital, insurance, governance and regulatory reporting obligations.

Do not claim that VYRA or Alitiora is licensed, regulated, insured, custodian-approved or broker-connected unless documentary evidence has been obtained and reviewed. Live execution remains disabled in code.
