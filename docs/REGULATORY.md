# Regulatory and operating gates

This repository is software development, not a brokerage launch authorization. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, asset, customer and business model. Before serving users, obtain qualified legal advice and determine licenses/registrations, partner model, KYC/AML, sanctions, privacy, consumer disclosures, complaints, capital, custody, safeguarding, reporting, tax and outsourcing obligations. Do not accept client money or claim broker status while these gates are unmet.
